# Bard's Hall

Play music and ambience from YouTube for everyone at the table. Paste a link, put it in a folder, press play. Several tracks can play at the same time, so you can layer a tavern, rain and a fire.

## Features
- YouTube links only, no account needed
- Folders with a color of your choice, plus a search box inside each folder
- Multiple tracks playing at once, each with its own volume and loop
- Every player has their own **master volume** slider, saved on their device
- Export and import your whole library as a JSON file

## How to use
1. The GM opens Bard's Hall from the toolbar and creates a folder.
2. Paste a YouTube link inside the folder and press play.
3. Everybody in the room hears it. Each player can lower their own master volume.

## Good to know
- The library is not stored in the room. It resets when the page reloads, so use **Export** to keep it and **Import** to bring it back.
- Every player needs the extension added to hear the music.
